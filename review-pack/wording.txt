Printed by scripts/copy-approval-list.mjs at the head of the contractor branch.

content/app.ts, content/coverLetterTemplates.ts and
lib/email/representativeInvite.ts. The disclaimers and the email footer line
are printed in emails.txt; the legal pages are in legal-pages.txt.

Since the run-6 print (421 items, now 428), run 7 added or changed:
  - the badge words: Sent, Opened, Downloaded, Could not send, Bounced,
    Link stopped, Expired, Scheduled, and "Status" (the name a screen reader
    hears before them). The badges show them in capitals; the capitals come
    from the styling, so the stored words stay in sentence case;
  - the deliveries page's status words, now "Could not send" and "Opened"
    (were "Couldn't be sent" and "Link opened");
  - "Deleted", before the moment on each deleted packet;
  - two examples: "Contractor license — Texas" (was "RRT license — Texas")
    and "Prequalification — Harbor Street project" (was "Travel assignment —
    Denver").

One heading below, "Other — packages (content/app.ts)", is built from the
name of the code section the strings live in. No user reads it.
