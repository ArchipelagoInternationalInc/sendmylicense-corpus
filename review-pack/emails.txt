STEELTICKET — EVERY EMAIL THE APP SENDS, VERBATIM
Printed 2026-09-25 from the contractor branch by scripts/review-pack.ts.

Each email is printed from the real template code with example values.
Example values used: sender 'Example Framing Co.' / 'Site Office', packet
'Prequalification packet', recipient estimating@example.com, and links under
steelticket.example. Real links carry a random token.

Each email has two parts: plain text and HTML. Both are printed. The HTML part
is shown with its markup removed; links are shown as [link: …].

==============================================================================
1. Reminder about a date the user entered
==============================================================================
When: Sent by the daily reminder job to the account holder (and to a helper who opted in to reminder copies).
From: The app's transactional sender (the From line is set outside the code; see the end of this file).
Source: lib/email/emailTemplates.ts (reminderEmail); called from lib/reminders/job.ts

SUBJECT: Reminder: State contractor license — 30 days until the date you entered

PLAIN-TEXT PART:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is a reminder you set up in Steelticket. For "State contractor license", the expiration date you entered is October 31, 2026 — 30 days from today.

You can review this credential and everything else in your vault on your dashboard.
Dashboard: https://steelticket.example/dashboard
Manage reminders: https://steelticket.example/credentials/example-id

It does not check your documents, interpret any contract or prequalification requirement, or confirm you are qualified for a job. ==============================================================================
THE FROM LINE
==============================================================================
The From line is the environment variable EMAIL_FROM, set outside the repository
(lib/email/resend.ts). On 2026-09-25 a test message sent by this branch arrived
from the display name "SendMyLicense" at a no-reply address on the old name's
mail subdomain. Changing it needs a new verified sending domain (owner item).
